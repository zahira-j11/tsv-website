"Our founder doesn't want to be on camera" comes up on our discovery calls all the time. Usually it's followed by a worry: if nobody from the company will front the content, can the brand still build a following on TikTok, Reels and Shorts?

Yes. Most of the accounts people follow on short-form aren't fronted by founders. They're fronted by someone the viewer recognises, doing a format the viewer recognises. That's something you can cast for.

## What creator-led organic is

Creator-led organic is one of our five formats. A creator from our network fronts a recurring series on your brand's own account. We match them to the audience you want to reach, then handle the strategy, scripting, filming, editing and posting.

You approve twice: first the concepts, scripts and casting, then the final videos. Nobody on your team has to appear on screen unless they want to.

## Why one consistent face matters

On the For You and Explore feeds, you aren't only competing with other brands. You're competing with every creator, meme page and celebrity your audience could watch instead. The accounts that win are the ones people recognise before they've read a word.

That's why we plan brand content the way successful creators plan theirs, not the way traditional brand accounts do. One recurring face in one recurring format becomes familiar in the scroll. Swap the presenter every week and you lose that.

So we usually recommend one face, or two, that your audience can get to know. If you want the feed to look broader, we add more creators in a month, but the core series keeps the same person.

## Formats that suit a creator-led series

**Storytime.** The creator tells a relatable story about a common problem, then your product comes in as the thing that would have helped. For a [finance app](/industries/fintech), we run a series where a presenter reads out money horror stories from Reddit. It highlights the pain point without a hard sell, which is why it suits categories where a direct pitch isn't appropriate.

**Plain-English explainers.** Short answers to the questions your customers actually search for. This is the content that keeps getting found months after it's posted.

**Games and choices.** "This or that" and "would you rather" formats keep people watching to the end, because the payoff only comes at the end.

**Lists and rankings.** The longer the list, the more people see themselves in it. For an edtech client, turning a "top 3 universities" video into a "top 40" took it from about 6,000 views to 10 million.

Whatever the format, we post it enough times to judge it fairly. Our [70/20/10 rule](/blog/70-20-10-content-rule) explains how we decide what to double down on.

## How we cast the creator

The right person on camera does more for a video than any edit. We cast from a network of 300+ vetted UK creators, actors and presenters, matched on age, life stage and how they speak, and on whether your audience would believe them.

You don't get a single name. We put forward more options than you need, with profiles and a self-tape, and you review them on our casting platform before anyone is booked. For an ongoing series we build a roster you've already approved, so a trend can be filmed without starting casting from zero. More detail is in [how we cast creators](/docs/creators).

## Will it still feel like our brand?

Yes, if the brand lives in the format rather than the face. The tone of voice, the topics, the look of the captions and the questions the creator asks all come from your brand guidelines, which we set up at onboarding. The creator brings the delivery. You approve the scripts before anything is filmed.

## When the founder should be on camera after all

Sometimes a founder or team member wants to appear, and that can work well alongside a creator-led series. We're happy to film with your team when they want to. The network is there for when they don't.

## Quick answers

**Can a brand grow on TikTok without the founder on camera?**
Yes. What builds a following is a recognisable person in a recognisable format, not who owns the company. A consistent creator fronting a recurring series does that job.

**What is creator-led organic content?**
A creator from an agency's network fronts a recurring series on the brand's own account. The agency handles strategy, scripting, filming, editing and posting, and the brand approves concepts and final videos.

**How many creators should a brand use?**
We usually recommend one face, or two, for the core series so the audience gets to know them. More creators can be added in a month if you want the feed to look broader.

**Who chooses the creator?**
You do. We put forward more options than you need, with profiles and a self-tape, and you approve who is on screen before we film.

**Can our own team be on camera too?**
Yes. We can film with your team whenever they want to appear, alongside or instead of a creator.
