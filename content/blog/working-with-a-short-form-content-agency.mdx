The agency comes up with the ideas. Your team sets the guardrails at the start, then signs off twice: the concepts before we film, and the finished videos before anything goes live.

It's the question we hear most on discovery calls, usually from in-house teams who are already stretched. Here's how it works in practice, using our own process. You don't need a content plan, a brief or anyone on camera to start.

## You can start from nothing

A brief can be one line. "Make our community famous on social" is enough for us to come back with formats to test.

## Step 1: the onboarding form

Before anything is made, you fill in an onboarding form. It covers your brand guidelines, the do-nots, the styles you don't like and anything that can't be said. If you're a [regulated brand](/docs/regulated-brands), this is where compliance guardrails and disclaimer lines go.

## Step 2: we build the strategy

We write the first strategy from that form. If there are themes you want covered, name them. If not, we start from what performs in your category and learn from there. Month one is about learning your voice, so the content sharpens as the weeks go on.

## Step 3: your account manager pitches the month

A dedicated account manager takes you through the strategy, the concepts and the first copy. For [street interviews](/street-interviews), that includes the questions. You approve the concepts, scripts and casting, so you know who will be on screen before we film.

## Step 4: we film and edit

We handle the location, the creators, the filming and the editing, using our network of 300+ vetted UK creators. Your team never has to find, brief or chase anyone. A street interview shoot takes about four hours and covers 20 to 25 people.

## Step 5: you approve the finished videos

You see every video before it goes live. That's the second sign-off, and nothing is posted without it.

## How long it takes

Onboarding, including the strategy, takes about two weeks. Filming is usually the week after. The first video is ready about two days after the shoot, and the rest arrive on a rolling basis through the month. Most brands have their first content live within 14 to 21 days of signing.

## What your team actually does

- Fills in the onboarding form once.
- Joins two calls a month: one to see the plan, one to go through the numbers.
- Signs off the concepts before filming and the videos before posting.

The full month is on [how a retainer runs](/docs/how-we-work).

## Quick answers

**Who comes up with the content ideas when you hire an agency?**
The agency does. At The Social Vision, we build the strategy and concepts from your onboarding form, and your team approves them before anything is filmed.

**Do we need a brief or a content plan to start?**
No. A one-line brief is enough. We come back with formats to test, and month one is about learning your voice.

**What does our team have to sign off?**
Two things: the concepts, scripts and casting before filming, and the finished videos before they go live.

**How long until the first videos are live?**
Usually within 14 to 21 days of signing: about two weeks of onboarding and strategy, filming the week after, and the first edit about two days after the shoot.

**Can we start if we've barely posted before?**
Yes. Habito hadn't posted for over a year when we started, and reached 25 million organic views in 8 months with zero ad spend.
