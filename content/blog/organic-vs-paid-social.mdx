You don't have to choose between them. Organic social shows you which videos people actually watch and share. Paid social puts budget behind those winners, and with the right usage rights you can run any of them as ads from your own brand account.

Most in-house teams we speak to run the two separately: one team posts, another buys media, and the creative rarely moves between them. Here's how to join them up.

## Organic vs paid social, in one line each

**Organic** is what you post on your own channels. Reach is free but slower, and it tells you what your audience genuinely cares about. [Habito](/case-studies/habito) reached 25 million organic views in 8 months with zero ad spend.

**Paid** is reach you buy. It's fast and targetable, but it eats creative: the same ad wears out quickly, so the paid team needs new versions all the time. [Plum](/case-studies/plum) uses us as its paid social creative partner, with three times the creative output and no creator management on its side.

## Why the best teams run both

Organic is the testing ground. Every post is a small, free experiment, and the ones that earn shares and watch time are concepts your audience has already approved. Paid is how you scale them. Spending behind a proven video is a safer bet than spending behind one nobody has seen.

That's the thinking behind our [70/20/10 rule](/blog/70-20-10-content-rule): most of what we make doubles down on formats that work, and the rest tests new ones.

## How to move a video from organic to paid

1. **Pick on shares and watch time, not views.** A video people [share](/blog/shares-beat-views) is a stronger signal than one that got lucky in the feed.
2. **Make variations.** We cut at least two hook variations per piece, so one concept becomes several tests.
3. **Run it from your brand account.** Your team or your media agency runs the campaign and controls the spend. We make the creative.
4. **Feed the results back.** What wins in paid tells us what to make more of in organic next month.

## Usage rights: the question to settle first

None of this works if the rights don't allow it. With The Social Vision, every video comes with perpetual usage rights, including paid use from your own brand account, so you can put ad spend behind any of it.

The exception is an ad that runs under the creator's name or handle: a TikTok Spark Ad from the creator's own post, whitelisting through the creator's account, or a Meta partnership ad. Those need a separate written agreement and the creator's authorisation, because the name and the account are theirs. The detail is on [usage rights](/docs/ugc-usage-rights) and [partnership ads](/docs/partnership-ads).

If you're comparing agencies, ask these before you sign:

- Are the usage rights perpetual, or do they expire?
- Can we run the content as ads from our own account?
- What does a creator-handle ad need, and who arranges it?

There are more questions in [how to choose a short-form content agency](/blog/how-to-choose-a-short-form-content-agency).

## Quick answers

**What's the difference between organic and paid social?**
Organic is content you post on your own channels for free reach. Paid is reach you buy with ads. Organic tells you what works; paid scales it.

**Should we start with organic or paid social?**
Run both, but let organic lead the creative. Videos that earn shares and watch time organically are the safest ones to put ad spend behind.

**Can we run our organic TikToks as paid ads?**
Yes, if your usage rights allow it. Content from The Social Vision comes with perpetual usage rights, including ads from your own brand account.

**Do Spark Ads need the creator's permission?**
If the Spark Ad runs from the creator's own post or handle, yes: that needs the creator's authorisation and a separate written agreement. Ads from your brand account's own posts don't.

**Do we need separate agencies for organic and paid creative?**
No. We make organic content and paid creative (Paid Social Productions and Remote Creator UGC) under one retainer, so winning videos move between them. Your team or media agency runs the campaigns.
